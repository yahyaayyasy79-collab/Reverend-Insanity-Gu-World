# ART — style guide and status

All art is original and generated by `tools/gen_art.py` from geometric primitives (`tools/art/`); nothing is traced
or copied from the novel, its translations, the manhua or fan art (MOD_SPEC rule 6). Placeholders
(`tools/make_placeholders.py`) remain for objects without a design yet.

## Icons (Unciv's flat style)

* 100x100, transparent, one colour, a bold silhouette filling about 80% of the canvas; Unciv draws it on a coloured
  circle. White for units, nations, techs, promotions, policies; black for buildings, resources, improvements (as in
  the base game's own atlases).
* Motifs: the **Gu beetle** (oval shell with a seam, six legs) marks Gu; **crescents** the Moon path; **fists and boar
  tusks** the Strength path; **lightning** the wolf tide; **rank diamonds** along the bottom of Gu Master icons (one per
  rank); **primeval stones** as faceted gems; Qing Mao's peaks for the Gu Yue clan.
* Every Slice 1 object has a designed icon (89; `tools/art/glyphs.py` holds the first 19, `glyphs2.py` the rest);
  18 objects keep the base game's own icon (Farm, Mine, Road, Furs and other engine-coupled Vanilla copies).
  Approved by the user on the sample sheet (2026-10-17: "lanjut ke semua").
* Category circles in the contact sheet (`tools/gen_art.py --sheet FILE`) only mimic the game; Unciv picks the real
  circle colours (civ colours for units and nations).

## Units: pixel art (2026-11-02)

The user asked for every unit to be redrawn, closer to the novel, in the pixel style of the HexaRealm (64 × 56) and
FantasyHex (32 × 28) tilesets. `tools/art/pixelunits.py` draws all 43 units natively at both sizes from one scene
written in HexaRealm pixels (the mask, shading and outline helpers of `pixeltiles.py`). The 3D renders before them
(`render3d.py`, `characters.py`) now only draw the leaders' ink silhouettes.

* **Map sprites, no frame.** The engine scales a unit image over the whole hex, like a tile
  [src `TileLayerUnitSprite`], so a grass frame on a sprite would paint grass over desert, snow, sea or a city, and
  hide resources and roads. The user saw a mockup and chose: no frame on the map, the frame on the portraits. As on
  the tilesets' own units, each sprite is a figure and a soft ground shadow on a transparent canvas: soldiers on the
  right half, civilians (Clan Settler, Mortal Laborer) on the left, so two units on one tile do not overlap. Tall
  subjects (immortals, Venerable, the wolf king, desolate beasts, Gu Houses) rise above the hex in a taller image.
* **Every unit set.** The 64 × 56 sprite goes to the default set (AbsoluteUnits) and to HexaRealm's, the 32 × 28 one to
  FantasyHex's, so the units show whichever unit set the player picks (Options → Display).
* **Clan colours.** `<unit>-1.png` (the robe) is greyscale and tinted with the nation's inner colour, `<unit>-2.png`
  (trim, sash, mantle, cape, banners) with its outer colour [src `getLayeredImageColored`]. Beasts have no clan
  colour, so they have no layers.
* **Canon cues.** Rank shows as the essence colour of the held Gu (green copper, red iron, white silver, yellow gold,
  purple crystal; immortal green grape, red date, white lychee, yellow apricot) and as regalia: rank 2 a pouch and
  headband, 3 a mantle, 4 a gold guan cap and an orbiting jade Gu, 5 a cape, a hairpin and a second Gu. The robes are
  cross-collared with wide sleeves. Gu Yue and the Moon line raise the Moonlight Gu's crescent; Bai the Stream Gu's
  water (its emblem, LORE_MAPPING); Xiong a bear phantom (Bear Strength Gu) and the Strength line a boar phantom (the
  Black-White Boar Gu), both bare-armed; Wu a wind. The Thunder Crown Wolf wears its canon crown of gold fur.
  Immortals float on cloud in their essence's ribbons, with a halo from rank seven; the Venerable stands in a
  yellow-apricot aura with dao marks around it.
* **ADAPTED designs.** The novel gives no look for these, so the designs are ours: the desolate beasts (a plated,
  horned boar-rhino; a frilled serpent; a winged colossus, each in its rank's essence aura); the four Gu Houses (a
  walking altar with red threads of luck, a flying war-chariot with amber time wheels, a palace on a floating rock
  with a jade dragon, the Heavenly Court's pagoda with Fate Gu's light at its top); the lone cultivator's bamboo
  hat, the demonic path's black robes and blood aura, and the immortal zombie's grey-green skin and stiff arms.
* **Portraits are dioramas** (`Images.Units/UnitPortraits/<unit>.png`, 144 × 144 = 72 × 72 pixels doubled): the unit
  stands on the tilesets' grass hex with its own ground inside the frame: a clan courtyard, Bai's stream, Xiong's
  rocks, clouds for immortals, a forest edge for wolves, ash and bones for beasts, a field for mortals. Portraits are
  never tinted, so each wears a fitting nation's colours. The Ink Night background (`Background.png`) is 144 × 144 to
  match (Portrait.kt scales by the ratio).
* **Attack effects** (`<unit>-attack-<n>.png`, default unit set, 64 × 55 pixels doubled to the old 128 × 110 frame):
  * the rank's essence strike, with more rings at higher ranks;
  * Moonlight crescent blades;
  * the boar or bear phantom charging;
  * Bai's water whip and Wu's wind blades;
  * lightning (gold for the wolf king) and beast claws;
  * blood slashes for the demonic path and ghost-green claws for the zombie;
  * one move per Gu House: luck threads, time ripples, a dream dragon, Fate's beam.
  Every unit has its own frames, so the old frames per unit type are gone.
* Limits: Unciv has no idle, walking or death animation; an attack effect plays on the unit that takes the damage.

## Sounds: Chinese instruments (2026-11-03)

The user asked for better, clearer sounds that stay readable, and chose the voice of Chinese instruments. All sounds
are synthesized from code by `tools/art/sfx.py` with instrument models, and ffmpeg encodes them as Ogg Vorbis (mono,
32 kHz). Nothing is sampled or copied. `tools/gen_art.py` writes them to `sounds/`. The build is bit-exact, so an
unchanged sound keeps its bytes. The file names did not change, so the rules still find them.

* **Instruments.**
  * Strings (Karplus-Strong with a wooden body): the warm guqin and the bright pipa.
  * Winds (additive, with breath noise): the dizi, with its membrane's buzz, and the soft xiao.
  * The bowed erhu, its harmonics shaped by formants.
  * Bronze: bianzhong two-tone bells, gongs whose pitch sinks (the great gong) or rises (the small gong), stone
    chimes, nao/bo cymbals.
  * Tanggu and dagu drums, and muyu woodblocks.
  * Melodic notes keep to the pentatonic scale on D.
* **Mixing.** Every sound gets a hall reverb (early reflections, a tail whose highs die first), a 45 Hz low cut and a
  9 kHz top cut, and a soft limiter. Loudness is set per kind by RMS, measured above 180 Hz as a phone speaker plays
  it, so a bass-heavy drum is not too quiet on Android.
  * Rank body: -17 dB.
  * Paths: -19 to -16 dB by tier.
  * Dao bells: -24 to -20 dB, soft, since they ring on every attack.
  * Killer moves and combos: -16 dB.

An attack layers:

| Layer | File | Plays when | Scales with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | `gu-rank-<1..9>` | the unit's `attackSound`, every attack | rank: a tanggu for rank one, growing to the great dagu; immortals add a gong; the Venerable a sinking great gong and a chord of bronze bells |
| Path | `path-<key>-<1..4>` | Play-sound unique on the attainment tier held, on each attack (only the highest tier plays) | attainment: Master is the path's own instrument (47 recipes); Grandmaster adds an echo strike; Great Grandmaster a great drum and a hall; Supreme a gong, a shimmer of bells and a long tail |
| Dao | `dao-<1..5>` | Play-sound unique on Dao Marks I-V, on each attack | dao marks: one small bianzhong bell up to a chord of three |
| Gu attack | `move-<key>`, `move-<key>-immortal` | Play-sound unique when a killer move is used | a cymbal roll swelling into the Great Grandmaster or Supreme strike; the immortal move adds a great gong |

The Gu catalog's awakening sounds (`gu-<name>`) give each Gu its own voice before its path's strike:

* winged Gu: a pipa tremolo;
* crawling Gu: woodblock clicks;
* crystal and flower Gu: a chime;
* blade and shell Gu: a small gong;
* flowing Gu: a xiao's breath.

Immortal Gu add the dao bells. The combination moves (`combo-<name>`) play both Gu's path strikes a beat apart after
a cymbal swell.

Each path's instrument names its character. Some examples:

* strength: the great drum and a low gong;
* sword: a swish into a high pipa note and a chime;
* water: a guqin note and water drops;
* wind: a dizi's swoop;
* moon: a xiao note and a bell;
* time: woodblock tick-tock and a bell;
* heaven: a chord of bells over the great gong;
* dream: a guzheng-like run;
* luck: tinkling chimes.

The recipes are `path_core()` in `sfx.py`. Play-sound uniques sound only for a human player (CAPABILITIES E11).
`check_refs` fails on any sound the rules name without a file, and warns on files nothing plays.

## Jewel enamel and gold: portraits, splash screens, leaders, victories (2026-11-03)

The user asked for every image except the units, the terrain and the attack effects to be redone: luxurious, simple,
and readable even when small. From a three-way study (A black lacquer and gold, B jewel enamel and gold, C white jade
and gold) the user chose B. The Ink Night pass below is the history it replaces.

* **Portraits** (1,002, 96 px, `portrait.enamel` in `tools/art/portrait.py`, built by `tools/art/inkpass.py`): a disc
  of enamel in the object's own colour, brighter toward the top left with a soft gloss. The object's designed shape is
  in gold relief on it, with a drop shadow and a dark outline, inside a thin gold bezel.
  * There is no texture, brush stroke or seal.
  * The shape fills 70% of the disc, up from 62%, so it reads at 32 px.
  * The colour carries the meaning, as before: a path's family colour, the era's essence colour for techs, the
    nation's colour, amber for hunger, red for debuffs.
* **Backgrounds.** Each type's `Background.png` is only a soft shadow now: Unciv still draws the portrait at full size
  [src ui/images/Portrait.kt], and the medallion carries its own colour. The units' diorama portraits get the same
  shadow.
* **Wonder splash screens** (87, 256 × 128): an enamel dusk in the wonder's colour, a gold sun in a ring, three ridges
  each edged in gold, the wonder's medallion, a double gold border.
* **Leaders** (8): the leader's figure in gold relief on an enamel medallion in the nation's colour, a pale moon behind.
* **Victories:** the same layered scenes as before, in flat enamel skies and ridges with every crest and edge in gold,
  gold stars and moons:
  * a crimson dusk for Supremacy, deep teal for Endurance, royal purple for Eternal Life;
  * banners, sundial, gate, river and stair in gold and red enamel;
  * won is golden rays and a gold frame, lost a dark veil with a broken gold ring.

  The tab icons are medallions too.
* **Unit types, the primeval stone and the mod's banner.**
  * Unit type icons are medallions.
  * The primeval-stone stat icon is a jade gem in a gold setting.
  * The mod manager's banner (`preview.png`) has the Spring Autumn Cicada's medallion, the title in gold, and the
    four signature paths' medallions on jade enamel.

## Ink Night (Phase D, 2026-11-02; replaced by enamel and gold on 2026-11-03)

The user asked for the whole mod's look to be "100x better" and picked style D, Ink Night, from a four-way style
study (ink and jade, bronze medallion, spirit glow, ink night), with the full package.

* **Portraits.** Every building, tech, resource, improvement, promotion and nation now has a full-colour portrait, 1,002
  in all (`Images.Portraits/<Type>Portraits/`, 96 px, `tools/art/portrait.py` + `tools/art/inkpass.py`). Each one is
  built from the object's own designed silhouette:
  * the shape is lit as a relief from a height map and glows in the object's colour, on a soft halo;
  * objects of a path carry a small red seal with the path's symbol.

  Colours: a path's family colour for everything of that path, the era's essence colour (Eras.json `iconRGB`) for
  techs, the nation's brighter colour for nations, warm amber for hunger meters, red for debuff statuses.
* **Backgrounds.** Each type has a `Background.png` at the portrait's size: an ink disc with a brush circle (ensō),
  tinted per type (buildings gold, techs blue, resources green, improvements earth, promotions and nations neutral).
  Unciv then draws the portrait at full size instead of shrinking it to 75% inside its white circle
  [src ui/images/Portrait.kt]. Because the disc lives once per type and not in every portrait, the portraits pack into
  8.9 MB of atlas instead of 18.8 MB.
* **Units** keep their 3D portraits, now on the Ink Night disc (`Images.Units/UnitPortraits/Background.png`, 128 px).
* **Wonder splash screens.** All 87 wonders have one (`Images.Wonders/WonderImages/`, 256 × 128): a misty ink
  landscape of layered ridges at night with a moon, and the wonder's portrait in its ensō.
* **Leaders.** The nine majors' leaders are ink silhouettes, rim-lit in the nation's colour, before a great moon
  inside an ensō, each with its clan's path signs (moon blades, water rings, an immortal's halo) and seal
  (`Images.Victories/LeaderIcons/`, in the Victories atlas since Layer 13: a ninth leader overflowed game.png).
* **Victories** (fixed the same day: the user saw "only the white disc"). Unciv shows the `Background` always and stacks
  each reached milestone, `Won` or `Lost` over it [src VictoryScreenIllustrations]. The first version's background
  was an empty ensō, so that was all a new game showed. Now each victory is a layered scene (`tools/art/victories.py`):
  * Supremacy: Qing Mao's ridges at night with four lit clan villages; conquest raises banners and fires over them;
  * Endurance: the golden river of time past its waymarks; the milestone lights the great sundial;
  * Eternal Life: the heavenly stair into the clouds; the milestone raises the gate of eternal life;
  * won and lost, for every victory: won adds a golden dawn and frame, lost an ink veil with a broken ensō.

  Each victory also has a tab icon (`VictoryTypeIcons`).
* **Unit types.** Civilopedia shows an icon for every unit type (`Images/UnitTypeIcons/`).
* **Policies stay flat white**, because Unciv tints policy icons by state (pickable, adopted)
  [src PolicyPickerScreen]: coloured art would hide that state.
* **Sources are not committed.** `Images.Portraits`, `Images.Wonders` and `Images.Victories` are regenerated by
  `tools/gen_art.py` (about 4.5 minutes, four processes). Only their atlases (`Portraits*`, `Wonders`, `Victories`) are
  committed, since Android reads the atlas and never the sources. `tools/unciv_ci.py --pack` refuses to pack while
  they are missing, so no pack can silently drop them.

## Units and the mod's terrain, second pass (2026-11-02)

The user asked for the units and the mod's own terrain to be "100x better".

* **Units on the map are about 20% larger** (render scale 4.4 to 3.7), and each rank has its own silhouette
  (`characters.regalia`, stacking):
  * rank 2: a Gu pouch and a headband;
  * rank 3: a shoulder mantle in the clan's outer colour;
  * rank 4: a guan cap and an orbiting jade Gu;
  * rank 5: a long cape in the clan's colour, a gold hairpin and a second Gu;
  * immortals: ribbons of their rank's essence, on top of the cloud and halos they already had.

  The strength path (bare-chested) keeps its own build. Before this, ranks 1-5 differed only by the colour of the orb.
* **The twelve secret realms and the five region walls are pixel-art dioramas** (`tools/art/pixeltiles.py`, redrawn
  2026-11-02 at the user's request; the 3D renders before them blurred when reduced). They follow the two tilesets'
  own style: a three-quarter top view on a hexagonal grass frame whose lower sides show the slab (a dark green band
  and a black edge on HexaRealm, brown earth on FantasyHex), with the hex rows and frame colours of their Grassland.
  * Each tile is drawn natively at both sizes, HexaRealm 64 × 56 and FantasyHex 32 × 28, from one scene written in
    HexaRealm pixels; nothing is scaled down. Tall subjects (peaks, gates, walls) rise above the hex in a taller
    image (64 × 60 to 64 × 70), as HexaRealm's own Mountain (64 × 64) does: the engine sizes a tile image by its
    width and lets the extra height stand above the hex [src TileLayer.setHexagonSize].
  * Shapes take five-tone ramps (a darker same-hue outline, shadow, mid, light, highlight), are lit from the top
    left and drop a shadow to the bottom right. Mountains are heightfields rendered front to back like a voxel
    landscape, so ridges, gullies, cliff faces and snow come from real slopes; at 32 × 28 their tones are flat (no
    dither) so they stay legible.
  * Realms: Dang Hun's massif in a ring of soul light; Luo Po's V-shaped gorge with a pale whirlwind; Reverse Flow's
    river climbing a terraced hill to a spout; the golden ring river of time around an hourglass; the white and
    black Door of Life and Death; stacked books under an open book; Crazed Demon Cave's crimson crag with a red-eyed
    mouth; Dragon Whale Paradise as an atoll whose lagoon holds the horned whale; Tian Ti's stair to a summit gate;
    Lang Ya's island over a sea of cloud; the Imperial Court's golden tent; Hu Immortal's shrine, peach blossoms
    and white fox.
  * Walls: a water curtain with foam, a rampart of fire, a poison fog bank with dead trees, tangled licorice roots,
    and a stepped stone rampart with jade runes.
