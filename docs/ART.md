# ART — style guide and status

All art is original and generated by `tools/gen_art.py` from geometric primitives (`tools/art/`); nothing is traced
or copied from the novel, its translations, the manhua or fan art (MOD_SPEC rule 6). Placeholders
(`tools/make_placeholders.py`) remain for objects without a design yet.

## Icons (Unciv's flat style)

* 100x100, transparent, one colour, a bold silhouette filling about 80% of the canvas; Unciv draws it on a coloured
  circle. White for units, nations, techs, promotions, policies; black for buildings, resources, improvements (as in
  the base game's own atlases).
* Motifs: the **Gu beetle** (oval shell with a seam, six legs) marks Gu; **crescents** the Moon path; **fists and boar
  tusks** the Strength path; **lightning** the wolf tide; **rank diamonds** along the bottom of Gu Master icons (one per
  rank); **primeval stones** as faceted gems; Qing Mao's peaks for the Gu Yue clan.
* Every Slice 1 object has a designed icon (89; `tools/art/glyphs.py` holds the first 19, `glyphs2.py` the rest);
  18 objects keep the base game's own icon (Farm, Mine, Road, Furs and other engine-coupled Vanilla copies).
  Approved by the user on the sample sheet (2026-10-17: "lanjut ke semua").
* Category circles in the contact sheet (`tools/gen_art.py --sheet FILE`) only mimic the game; Unciv picks the real
  circle colours (civ colours for units and nations).

## Attack animations

Played over the unit that takes damage, chosen by the attacker's unit type [docs Creating-a-custom-tileset.md]; pixel
art on a 32x32 grid scaled 3x to match the default AbsoluteUnits unit set.

| Attacker type | Animation | Colours | Sound (`attackSound`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gu Master (rank one) | burst of primeval essence | green copper (rank-one essence) | throw |
| Moon Gu Master | a moon blade sweeps and shatters into moonlight | silver-blue | whoosh |
| Strength Gu Master | boar tusks slam, impact star, shock rings | earth and amber | nonmetalhit |
| Beast (lightning wolves) | bolt from above, flash, sparks | electric blue | shot |

## 3D characters (pre-rendered)

Unciv draws 2D images only, so the twelve units are modelled in 3D and pre-rendered by `tools/art/render3d.py` (a
numpy ray-marcher over signed distance fields: spheres, capsules, round cones, ellipsoids, tori, crescent blades;
three-band toon shading, rim light, specular on metal and jade, outlines, glow on Gu effects) from scenes in
`tools/art/characters.py`.

* **Map sprites** `Images.Units/TileSets/AbsoluteUnits/Units/<unit>.png` (the default unit set; `Images.Units` packs
  into its own atlas, `Units.png`, so the icons keep `game.png` to one page), 128x110, three-quarter
  view with a ground shadow, plus `<unit>-1.png` (robe, tinted with the clan's inner colour) and `<unit>-2.png`
  (sash, collar, banner: the outer colour) [src `TileLayerUnitSprite`, `getLayeredImageColored`].
* **Portraits** `Images.Units/UnitPortraits/<unit>.png` (the Units atlas), 128x128, head and shoulders, robe in the path's colour; Unciv uses
  them everywhere except the world map [docs Images-and-Audio.md].
* **Attack effects** `<unit>-attack-<n>.png`, chosen by the attacker's unit name: rank-one essence burst; Moon: one,
  two or three moon blades by rank; Strength: an essence boar charge and shock rings in the rank's essence colour;
  wolves: a lightning strike (golden for the Thunder Crown Wolf). The pixel frames per unit type stay as fallback.
* **Rank reads as essence** (canon colours): a ring at the feet in green copper (rank one), red iron (two), white
  silver (three), yellow gold (four); Strength masters grow broader and gain bracers and boar-skull pauldrons; Moon
  masters gain blades and, at rank four, a moon behind the back.
* **Rank strikes** (Phase D, 2026-11-02, `characters.strike_frame`). The generic rank units, the immortals, the
  Venerable, the desolate beasts, the Gu Houses and the immortal zombie strike in their rank's essence colour, and the
  strike grows with rank:
  * ranks 1–2: a burst and sparks, 3 frames;
  * ranks 3–4: a shock ring;
  * rank 5: two rings;
  * rank 6 (immortal): a qi pillar rises, 5 frames;
  * rank 7: rays tear outward;
  * rank 8 (grotto-heaven): a halo opens above;
  * rank 9 (Venerable): four rings, a crown of light and a ground shockwave, 6 frames.
  The clan chains keep their path's own strike (moon blades, water, boar charge, whirlwind).
* Limits: Unciv has no idle or walking animation and no death animation; an attack effect plays when a unit takes
  damage, including the killing blow. The effect follows the attacker's unit (path and rank), not the Gu it holds
  [src `BattleTableHelpers.getAttackAnimationLocation`].

## Sounds (Phase D, 2026-11-02)

All sounds are synthesized from code by `tools/art/sfx.py`: numpy builds them from noise, tones, metallic partials,
plucked strings and clicks, and ffmpeg encodes them as Ogg Vorbis (mono, 22 kHz, about 2 MB in all). Nothing is
sampled or copied. `tools/gen_art.py` writes them to `sounds/`. The build is bit-exact, so an unchanged sound keeps
its bytes. An attack layers:

| Layer | File | Plays when | Scales with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | `gu-rank-<1..9>` | the unit's `attackSound`, every attack | rank: deeper, longer, wider; immortals add a qi hum, the Venerable a choir and thunder |
| Path | `path-<key>-<1..4>` | Play-sound unique on the attainment tier held, on each attack (only the highest tier plays) | attainment: Master is the path's own timbre (47 recipes); Grandmaster adds an echo strike; Great Grandmaster a sub thump and space; Supreme a shimmer and a long tail |
| Dao | `dao-<1..5>` | Play-sound unique on Dao Marks I-V, on each attack | dao marks: one bell, brighter and fuller up to a three-bell chord |
| Gu attack | `move-<key>`, `move-<key>-immortal` | Play-sound unique when a killer move is used | a rising swell into the Great Grandmaster or Supreme strike; the immortal move adds a falling sub drop |

Each path's timbre names its character. Some examples:

* fire: whoosh and crackle;
* ice: crystal ping and crack;
* lightning: falling FM zap and sparks;
* sword: bright swish and ring;
* sound: plucked qin and gong;
* time: tick-tock and swell;
* luck: coin chimes;
* heaven: chord and thunder.

The recipes are the `PATHS` table in `sfx.py`. Play-sound uniques sound only for a human player (CAPABILITIES E11).
`check_refs` fails on any sound the rules name without a file, and warns on files nothing plays.

## Ink Night: portraits, splash screens, leaders, victories (Phase D, 2026-11-02)

The user asked for the whole mod's look to be "100x better" and picked style D, Ink Night, from a four-way style
study (ink and jade, bronze medallion, spirit glow, ink night), with the full package.

* **Portraits.** Every building, tech, resource, improvement, promotion and nation now has a full-colour portrait, 1,002
  in all (`Images.Portraits/<Type>Portraits/`, 96 px, `tools/art/portrait.py` + `tools/art/inkpass.py`). Each one is
  built from the object's own designed silhouette:
  * the shape is lit as a relief from a height map and glows in the object's colour, on a soft halo;
  * objects of a path carry a small red seal with the path's symbol.

  Colours: a path's family colour for everything of that path, the era's essence colour (Eras.json `iconRGB`) for
  techs, the nation's brighter colour for nations, warm amber for hunger meters, red for debuff statuses.
* **Backgrounds.** Each type has a `Background.png` at the portrait's size: an ink disc with a brush circle (ensō),
  tinted per type (buildings gold, techs blue, resources green, improvements earth, promotions and nations neutral).
  Unciv then draws the portrait at full size instead of shrinking it to 75% inside its white circle
  [src ui/images/Portrait.kt]. Because the disc lives once per type and not in every portrait, the portraits pack into
  8.9 MB of atlas instead of 18.8 MB.
* **Units** keep their 3D portraits, now on the Ink Night disc (`Images.Units/UnitPortraits/Background.png`, 128 px).
* **Wonder splash screens.** All 87 wonders have one (`Images.Wonders/WonderImages/`, 256 × 128): a misty ink
  landscape of layered ridges at night with a moon, and the wonder's portrait in its ensō.
* **Leaders.** The eight majors' leaders are ink silhouettes, rim-lit in the nation's colour, before a great moon
  inside an ensō, each with its clan's path signs (moon blades, water rings, an immortal's halo) and seal
  (`Images/LeaderIcons/`).
* **Victories** (fixed the same day: the user saw "only the white disc"). Unciv shows the `Background` always and stacks
  each reached milestone, `Won` or `Lost` over it [src VictoryScreenIllustrations]. The first version's background
  was an empty ensō, so that was all a new game showed. Now each victory is a layered scene (`tools/art/victories.py`):
  * Supremacy: Qing Mao's ridges at night with four lit clan villages; conquest raises banners and fires over them;
  * Endurance: the golden river of time past its waymarks; the milestone lights the great sundial;
  * Eternal Life: the heavenly stair into the clouds; the milestone raises the gate of eternal life;
  * won and lost, for every victory: won adds a golden dawn and frame, lost an ink veil with a broken ensō.

  Each victory also has a tab icon (`VictoryTypeIcons`).
* **Unit types.** Civilopedia shows an icon for every unit type (`Images/UnitTypeIcons/`).
* **Policies stay flat white**, because Unciv tints policy icons by state (pickable, adopted)
  [src PolicyPickerScreen]: coloured art would hide that state.
* **Sources are not committed.** `Images.Portraits`, `Images.Wonders` and `Images.Victories` are regenerated by
  `tools/gen_art.py` (about 4.5 minutes, four processes). Only their atlases (`Portraits*`, `Wonders`, `Victories`) are
  committed, since Android reads the atlas and never the sources. `tools/unciv_ci.py --pack` refuses to pack while
  they are missing, so no pack can silently drop them.

## Units and the mod's terrain, second pass (2026-11-02)

The user asked for the units and the mod's own terrain to be "100x better".

* **Units on the map are about 20% larger** (render scale 4.4 to 3.7), and each rank has its own silhouette
  (`characters.regalia`, stacking):
  * rank 2: a Gu pouch and a headband;
  * rank 3: a shoulder mantle in the clan's outer colour;
  * rank 4: a guan cap and an orbiting jade Gu;
  * rank 5: a long cape in the clan's colour, a gold hairpin and a second Gu;
  * immortals: ribbons of their rank's essence, on top of the cloud and halos they already had.

  The strength path (bare-chested) keeps its own build. Before this, ranks 1-5 differed only by the colour of the orb.
* **The twelve secret realms and the five region walls are small 3D dioramas** (`tools/art/terrain3d.py`). The flat
  clip art is replaced by a scene of primitives rendered from a 36° three-quarter view, with a soft shadow, on a
  painted hex of its ground (grass, plains, ocean, rock, ash, swamp), then reduced to HexaRealm's 64 × 56 and
  FantasyHex's 32 × 28. Examples:
  * Dang Hun's violet soul rings;
  * the river climbing Reverse Flow's hill;
  * the golden river of time with its hourglass;
  * the white and black Door of Life and Death;
  * the whale with dragon horns;
  * Tian Ti's spiral stair through a cloud ring;
  * Lang Ya's floating island and furnace;
  * the walls: a breaking water curtain, layered flames, toxic fog with dead trees, giant tangled roots, and
    rune-cut stone.
