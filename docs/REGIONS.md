# The five regions as canon

The user (2026-11-05): each region's power, technology, politics and conflict must match the novel. The Central
Continent cannot be weaker than the Southern Border, since its order is millions of years old; its Heavenly Court
judges in heaven's name and does not like war. Immortals should fly over water, yet land units can still strike
them.

The canon research was done on 2026-11-08 by a subagent over the raw Chinese text, 2,335 chapters. Each fact below
is a verdict in our own words; no text of the novel is kept anywhere in the repository. Citations follow
`docs/CANON_REFERENCE.md` (R V#c# = volume and chapter of the raw text).

## Canon verdicts

### Corrections to the mod

* **Region walls stop the strong, not the weak.** Fang Yuan crossed as a rank-4 Gu Master, and crowds of mortal Gu
  Masters cross to the Refinement Conference. For immortals the crossing is hard at rank six and very dangerous at
  seven; rank eight almost never crosses and detours through the Black or White Heaven. Inside a wall an immortal is
  pushed and dragged, and its moves recoil on it (R V4c24, V4c61, V4c279, V5c535, V5c899, V4c286). The mod had this
  the other way round.
* Dou Shen Palace is written 豆神宫, a rank-8 Gu House of Genesis Lotus tied to the Qing clan (R V5c486-487).
* Feng Jiu Ge belongs to Spirit Affinity House, not the Court (R V4c163). Purple Mountain True Monarch belongs to the
  Shadow Sect (R V5c280). The Southern Border's Yang clan is 羊 (R V5c589).

### Power ranking

* The Central Continent is the strongest region, with the richest qi, the most sects and by far the most immortals.
  Its sect system takes in mortals, where clans train only their own blood (R V2c135, V4c55, V4c170, V5c704).
* The Northern Plains are the second fighting power, with the poorest land of the five and the most Gu Masters
  (R V3c153, V3c20, V4c282). Few rank 8s, because their wars kill immortals fastest (R V4c164).
* The Eastern Sea is the richest region and has the most rank 8s (R V4c243, V5c669).
* The Western Desert's resources are between the sea's and the plains'. It is the weakest late in the story: no
  Venerable leads it and it stays divided (R V5c475, V6c253).
* The Southern Border's rank against the sea and the desert is never stated. It has the most hermit immortals
  (R V5c192).
* The Heavenly Court dates from Remote Antiquity, about three million years ago (R V5c519, V6c35).

### Central Continent

* **The ten great sects.** Immortal Crane Sect, Heavenly Lotus Sect, Spirit Butterfly Valley, Ancient Soul Sect,
  Heaven's Envy Manor, Wind Cloud Manor, Combat Immortal Sect, Spirit Affinity House, Black Heaven Temple and Myriad
  Dragon Dock (R V6c358).
  * They are the Court's lower sects and hold about 80% of the continent's resources (R V4c307, V4c55).
  * They compete openly and in secret, through disciples' contests (R V4c56, V2c166).
  * The Court forbids them to attack other sects or lone immortals at will (R V4c56).
* **The Heavenly Court wages no ordinary war.**
  * Most of its immortals sleep in the Immortal Tomb and wake only in real need (R V5c49, V5c560).
  * Their apertures are part of the Court's grotto-heaven, so they face no calamities (R V5c55).
  * It acts against fate escapees, immortal zombies first; against threats to Fate Gu or Heaven's Will; against Red
    Lotus's true inheritances, attacks on its lower sects, and great treasures (R V4c131, V4c361, V5c406, V4c314,
    V5c213).
  * It plans wars of conquest only after the walls fall (R V5c314).
* Every ten years it issues the Demon Extermination List; every hundred years it backs the Refinement Conference,
  which draws refiners from all five regions (R V4c51, V4c179, V4c189).

### Northern Plains

* Tribes migrate for grass and clash constantly; they are the most mobile and warlike of all (R V3c20, V4c170).
* Big tribes swallow small ones, but a war needs a pretext (R V3c36-38, V3c73).
* The golden tribes 黄金家族 control almost everything and shut outsiders out (R V3c147, V5c84).
* **The Imperial Court contest** comes every ten years, before the killing blizzard (R V3c117, V3c97). Only mortal
  Gu Masters fight; immortals may help but never fight themselves (R V3c120, V3c124). The winners shelter in the
  Imperial Court Blessed Land and take its inheritances (R V3c84, V3c147).
* Longevity Heaven, Giant Sun's own grotto-heaven, rules the golden tribes; it is far weaker than the Court (R V5c123,
  V5c221).

### Eastern Sea

* Open sea and islands, counted in sea areas. No hegemon among many super forces, among them Tang, Shen, Song, Xia,
  Cai, Su, Nangong and Qing Yue; the Merman Imperial Court is one of them (R V2c135, V5c885, V4c336, V5c841).
* The lone and demonic immortals are the most numerous and the strongest of any region; Sea Market Blessed Land is
  their market (R V4c34; ch1650).
* The Zombie Alliance's headquarters ranks one notch above the local super forces (R V4c4).
* Every year the earth tide thins the Azure Water wall, and immortals of three regions slip in (R V4c170).

### Western Desert

* Oases like pearls in sand; the most trade-minded region (R V2c135, V3c20).
* A fight between Gu Masters risks wrecking an oasis, so caravans and cooperation are the norm. No one marches across
  the sand to take another oasis while their own holds (R V5c596, V3c20).
* There are fourteen peer super forces and no hegemon. Fang is not first, and the Wan clan leads a coalition to
  punish it (R V6c289, V5c757, V6c256).
* Flying over an oasis counts as a provocation (R V4c45). Demonic presence is thin (ch1708).

### Southern Border

* Thirteen righteous super clans, led by Wu, each ruling its own area. They annex one another's lands (R V5c589,
  V5c325).
* A hard land of mountains, beasts and wild Gu, with the most earth- and wood-path immortals (R V2c190, V5c331).
* The Shadow Sect's base (R V5c613).

### Between the regions

* The walls isolate the regions. Before the great age came a long peace in which immortals rarely fought to the death
  (R V4c18, V5c464).
* The regions meet through trade at Treasure Yellow Heaven, through the earth tide and through the Refinement
  Conference (R V4c17, V4c170, V4c189). Interventions are rare: the ten sects' covert army in the plains, and the
  Court at Yi Tian (R V4c42, V4c311).

## What the mod does (2026-11-08)

| Canon | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Immortals fly | `May travel on Water tiles without embarking`, `Ignores terrain cost` on ranks 6-8, the Venerable and the immortal zombie. Land melee units can still attack them over water, because embarkation is known from the first tech [src TargetHelper] |
| Walls stop the strong | Walls passable for everyone (movement cost 3, 10-15 damage per turn ended inside). Immortals take 10 / 30 / 60 more at ranks 6 / 7 / 8, desolate beasts likewise, Gu Houses as rank 8; the Venerable none |
| Central Continent strongest | The Court gets 6 free techs and two rank-three Gu Masters when it founds its capital, +15% science and production, and +50% city strength; the three sects on the map get +50% city strength |
| The Court wages no war | `declareWar` 0 (it never declares one, but fights back); it cannot seek Eternal Life |
| The Immortal Tomb | When a clan declares war on the Court, two rank-seven immortals wake (at most three in the field). They have no blessed land and burn their last Lifespan Gu |
| Northern Plains | 3 free techs and three rank-two Gu Masters, −15% stones, +25% military production, +3 happiness (so the engine's AI can declare war, E14), the most warlike temperament, and the Imperial Court Contest every ~13 turns |
| Eastern Sea | 3 free techs, +25% stones; the Fate Hunt on the Zombie Alliance while Fate Gu stands |
| Western Desert | 2 free techs, +20% stones, a trading and cooperative temperament |
| Southern Border | Unchanged: the baseline, and already at war |
